# SB-Interior-Work

### Hair Salon Interior Design
Project Overview

This project showcases the interior design and execution work completed for a hair salon by my brother and me. The work focused on creating a modern, attractive, and functional salon environment with stylish interiors and practical space utilization.

Work Description

- Executed modern interior design work for a hair salon, including decorative wall panels and wooden finishes.
- Installed and coordinated decorative lighting to enhance the salon's appearance and ambience.
- Worked on reception counter design featuring a marble-finish surface and customized wooden detailing
- Designed and constructed an interior partition to divide one room into separate functional spaces, improving space utilization, privacy, and overall interior layout..
- Contributed to interior finishing, furniture placement, and overall space organization.

Key Highlights

- Modern salon interior design
- Wooden wall paneling and decorative finishes
- Reception desk design and installation
- Lighting coordination and interior finishing

Project Type

Real-World Interior Design and Execution

Contribution

Hands-on involvement in completing the salon interior project in collaboration with my brother.
